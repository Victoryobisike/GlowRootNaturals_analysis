# GlowRoot Naturals Field Performance Report (April – May 2026)

![Header_image](<GlowRoot Naturals Header.jpg>)

## Executive Summary
GlowRoot Naturals logged **1,506** field activity entries between **6 April and 16 May 2026**, delivering **6,498** total completed task units across **15** retail outlets in Lagos. Overall execution averaged 26.37 minutes per log with a 76.94% quality score, and 75.0% of logged activities (1,130 entries) reached Completed status. However, one in four activity logs (376 entries) remained either Overdue (14.5%) or Pending (10.4%). 
Three activity patterns stand out. Field activity volume is heavily concentrated in **Yaba (1,164 tasks), Lekki (1,076 tasks), and Surulere (882 tasks)**, with Samples Distributed representing **72.0%** of total task output. Three promoters **(Ngozi Iwu, Segun Ogundipe, and Peter Osagie)** consistently take over 32.9 minutes per log while recording the lowest quality scores **(65.2%–65.7%)** and highest overdue rates **(25.9%–29.2%)**. Meanwhile, peripheral outlets such as **SPAR Ikorodu and SPAR Ajah** suffer from high overdue rates and below-average execution quality. Reallocating promoter capacity, coaching underperforming field staff, and tightening overdue follow-ups in outlying areas would substantially lift completion rates and campaign consistency.

## Business Context
GlowRoot Naturals is a skincare and haircare brand running an in-store promoter activation campaign across multiple retail outlets in Lagos. With promoters logging daily activity across different task types, statuses, and locations, the Trade Marketing team needs a clear, one-page Excel dashboard for Monday operational reviews to track where field effort is going, how efficiently promoters are working, and how much logged activity is actually getting completed.

## Objectives
1.	What are GlowRoot Naturals' headline completed task volume, average completion time, and average quality score?
2.	Which outlets has the most field effort and generates the most activity?
3.	Which promoters are working more efficiently and which take longer to complete tasks?
4.	How efficiently are promoters in each Area working?
5.	How much logged tasks are actually getting completed?


## Data Overview
The data is GlowRoot Naturals Field data, exported as an Excel file. It covers 36 active campaign days from **6 April to 16 May 2026** and contains **1506 rows** (one per logged-in activity) across **9 columns**. It spans 20 promoters,15 Outlets,10 areas, and 5 task types. Task completed, completion time, quality score and status are recorded for every activity.

## Key Findings
-	**6,498** total tasks were completed across **1,506** activity logs, averaging 26.37 minutes per log and a 76.94% quality score.
-	75.0% of activity logs (1,130) were Completed, while 14.5% (219) were Overdue and 10.4% (157) remained Pending.
-	Samples Distributed generated 72.0% of all task volume (4,681 units), whereas Product Demos required the longest average execution time (39.70 minutes).
-	**Justrite Yaba** (671 tasks), **Ebeano Supermarket Lekki** (665 tasks), and **Grand Square Ikeja** (592 tasks) led all outlets in total task volume, while Shoprite Ikeja recorded the lowest (116 tasks).
- Yaba was the top-performing area by volume (1,164 tasks; 17.9% of total) and completion rate (80.1%), whereas Ikorodu had the lowest completion rate (66.9%), highest overdue rate (21.0%), and lowest quality score (73.10%).
-	A sharp performance gap separates the fastest and slowest promoters: **Fatima Sule** averaged 21.39 minutes (83.92% quality), while **Ngozi Iwu** averaged 33.33 minutes (65.22% quality).

## Detailed Findings & Analysis
### Headline Performance 
Across 1,506 logged field entries, promoters delivered 6,498 completed task units at an overall pace of 26.37 minutes per log and an average quality score of 76.94%. Campaign momentum shifted between April (939 logs; 3,702 tasks; 76.3% completion rate) and May (567 logs; 2,796 tasks; 73.0% completion rate), with overdue incidence rising from 13.3% in April to 16.6% in May as task intensity per log increased.
### Activity Concentration (Outlets, Areas & Task Types) 
Task output is heavily skewed toward direct consumer sampling and top-tier retail outlets:
- Samples Distributed: 4,681 tasks (72.0% of total volume), averaging 19.23 minutes per log.
-	Sales Conversion: 821 tasks (12.6% of total volume), averaging 29.35 minutes per log.
-	Outlet Visit: 434 tasks (6.7% of total volume), averaging 24.79 minutes per log.
-	Product Demo: 353 tasks (5.4% of total volume), averaging 39.70 minutes per log.
-	Compliance Check: 209 tasks (3.2% of total volume), averaging 14.45 minutes per log.
By location, Justrite Yaba (671 tasks), Ebeano Supermarket Lekki (665 tasks), Grand Square Ikeja (592 tasks), SPAR Ikorodu (536 tasks), and Addide Supermarket Yaba (493 tasks) accounted for 45.5% of all completed tasks across the 15 outlets. At the area level, Yaba (1,164 tasks) and Lekki (1,076 tasks) led overall activity, whereas Onikan (242 tasks) and Alaba (303 tasks) generated the smallest activity. Notably, Shoprite Ikeja logged only 116 tasks across 38 entries, representing less than one-fifth of the output recorded at Grand Square Ikeja (592 tasks) in the same area.
### Promoter Efficiency 
Promoter performance divided into three distinct tiers across the 20-person field team:
-	Slowest Tier (Over 32 Mins): Ngozi Iwu (33.33 mins avg, 65.22% quality, 195 tasks), Segun Ogundipe (33.06 mins avg, 65.27% quality, 359 tasks), and Peter Osagie (32.95 mins avg, 65.72% quality, 332 tasks). All three recorded overdue rates between 25.9% and 29.2%, showing that longer task durations reflect execution friction rather than higher thoroughness.
-	Core Mid-Tier (24–29 Mins): 14 promoters clustered between 24.64 minutes (Bashir Yakubu) and 28.16 minutes (Temitope Owolabi), maintaining quality scores between 75.49% and 78.94%.
-	Fastest Tier (Under 24 Mins): Fatima Sule (21.39 mins avg, 83.92% quality, 498 tasks), Chuka Obi (23.56 mins avg, 84.50% quality, 91.7% completion rate), and Kelvin Eze (23.94 mins avg, 86.35% quality, 208 tasks) outperformed the team on both speed and execution quality.
Work Completion Health
-	**Completed**: 1,130 logs (75.0% of entries; 4,715 task units), averaging 25.61 minutes and 77.34% quality.
-	**Overdue**: 219 logs (14.5% of entries; 1,226 task units), averaging 28.62 minutes and 76.02% quality.
-	**Pending**: 157 logs (10.4% of entries; 557 task units), averaging 28.71 minutes and 75.39% quality.
Overdue backlogs are heavily localized. Ikorodu (21.0% overdue), Festac (19.2% overdue), Ajah (19.0% overdue), and Alaba (17.1% overdue) experienced nearly double the overdue rate of Onikan (9.4%), Marina (9.6%), and Yaba (10.8%).

## Recommendations
1.	**Coach and audit bottom-tier promoters**: Pair Ngozi Iwu, Segun Ogundipe, and Peter Osagie with top performers (Fatima Sule, Chuka Obi, and Kelvin Eze) for field shadowing, and set a 28-minute completion benchmark alongside a 75% minimum quality threshold.
2.	**Address outlying area bottlenecks**: Investigate operational delays in Ikorodu (SPAR Ikorodu), Ajah (SPAR Ajah and Ebeano Ajah), and Festac (Roban Stores Festac), where overdue rates exceed 19% and quality scores trail the Lagos average.
3.	**Rebalance Ikeja store coverage**: Reallocate promoter shifts from underutilized Shoprite Ikeja (116 tasks) toward high-converting outlets or pair visits with targeted product demos to boost store ROI.
4.	**Enforce end-of-day status closures**: Require promoters and area supervisors to clear or escalate Pending logs (10.4% of entries) before weekly Monday reviews so unfinished tasks do not roll over into Overdue status. 
5.	**Benchmark Product Demo workflows**: Standardize setup and customer engagement scripts for Product Demos (39.70 mins average) to trim unnecessary idle time without sacrificing conversion quality.

## Conclusion 

GlowRoot Naturals achieved strong field reach with 6,498 completed tasks and a 76.94% quality score, anchored by high-volume sampling in Yaba, Lekki, and Ikeja. However, a 25.0% combined Overdue and Pending rate, localized delays in areas like Ikorodu and Ajah, and significant speed-and-quality gaps among three promoters hold back overall campaign efficiency. Focusing supervision on underperforming promoters, tightening overdue tracking in outlying hubs, and balancing store coverage give the Trade Marketing team a direct path to higher completion health.
